# Colagens para ISR App — Prompts Midjourney/DALL-E

## Direção Geral
- **Estilo**: Mix de fotografias preto-e-branco + elementos gráficos coloridos sobrepostos
- **Cores**: Corais, roses, lavandas, verdes, tons quentes em fundos claros/pastéis
- **Pessoas**: Secundárias, pequenas, rostos em perfil ou silhuetas
- **Elementos**: Flores, plantas, formas geométricas, ocasionalmente arquitetura/ouro
- **Tom**: Editorial, sofisticado, artístico, profissional mas acessível

---

## 1. HERO / PRÓXIMO PASSO (Home)
**Tema**: Educação, crescimento, movimento para frente

```
A sophisticated collage artwork featuring:
- Black and white photograph of a young person (back view, small, in the corner) reading or taking notes
- Vibrant coral and rose flowers (peonies, roses) overlapping in the composition
- Geometric shapes in lavender and sage green (circles, wavy lines, rectangles)
- Golden/brass line elements (fine lines, brackets)
- Soft pastel background (cream/light beige)
- Editorial magazine aesthetic
- High quality, clean composition
- Aspect ratio: 4:3 or 16:9 (rectangular, mobile-friendly)
```

---

## 2. EXERCÍCIOS / ATIVIDADES (Home Cards)
**Tema**: Foco, movimento, progresso pessoal

```
A vibrant collage composition showing:
- Black and white image of hands writing or typing (small, positioned upper-left)
- Scattered botanical elements: green leaves, stems in watercolor style
- Rose and coral accent shapes (organic, flowing)
- Purple/lavender geometric accent bar (vertical, right side)
- Subtle gold circle element (background, soft)
- Clean, minimalist aesthetic
- Soft warm background
- Square or slightly rectangular aspect ratio (mobile card-sized)
```

---

## 3. FLIN (IA Practice) 
**Tema**: Tecnologia + natureza, inovação amigável

```
A modern collage featuring:
- Abstract geometric representation of AI/technology (digital patterns, clean lines in black)
- Vibrant lavender and rose color blocks
- Botanical elements: wildflowers, delicate stems in contrasting colors
- Small silhouette of a person (thinking pose, minimal detail)
- Sage green and coral accents
- Contemporary, tech-forward but warm aesthetic
- Cream/soft background
- Aspect ratio: square or 16:9
```

---

## 4. COMUNIDADE / DESAFIOS
**Tema**: Conexão, pessoas, comunidade

```
A beautiful collage artwork showing:
- Multiple small black and white faces/profiles (arranged in a circular composition, secondary elements)
- Large rose and coral flower clusters (prominent, vibrant)
- Connecting lines or geometric shapes linking the figures (suggesting connection)
- Lavender and green circular elements (background, soft)
- Gold fine-line details (elegant touches)
- Warm, inclusive, accessible feeling
- Soft pastel background
- Aspect ratio: square or slightly rectangular
```

---

## 5. AULAS (Top Section)
**Tema**: Estrutura, aprendizado, arquitetura

```
A sophisticated collage featuring:
- Black and white architectural element (column, building facade, subtle, background)
- Group of small silhouetted figures (studying, sitting, secondary)
- Rose, coral, and lavender paint splashes or watercolor elements
- Geometric shapes (rectangles, squares in soft colors)
- Fine gold line work (elegant framing)

- Aspect ratio: 16:9 or 4:3
```
