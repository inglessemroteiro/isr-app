# Prompts Finais para DALL-E — Colagens ISR App

**Base de Estilo Referência:** Vintage editorial collage com fundo verde escuro (teal/forest green), ilustrações botânicas douradas, pessoas, livros, elementos art deco.

---

## 1. HERO / PRÓXIMO PASSO — "Sua Aula é HOJE"
```
Vintage editorial collage on dark forest green background. Large open notebook with botanical illustrations (gold and cream). One young woman's face in profile (warm skin tone, focused expression, small scale on left). Sunflowers and delicate flowers in cream/gold. Butterflies or moths (illustrated style). Speech bubble with "Join the session" in cream. Art deco geometric elements. High quality, sophisticated, warm color palette. Aspect ratio 16:9 or 4:3 (rectangular).
```

## Generation Settings (DALL-E):
- **Style**: Vintage editorial collage, art deco illustration style
- **Background**: Dark forest green or dark teal (main color #2a5f5f or similar)
- **Accent Colors**: Cream/beige, gold (#d4a574), muted warm tones
- **Mood**: Warm, welcoming, sophisticated, nostalgic yet contemporary
- **Elements**: Botanical (flowers, leaves), people (illustrated), books, butterflies, geometric shapes, hand-lettered text
- **Quality**: HD, high detail, editorial magazine aesthetic
- **Format**: PNG, minimum 1200px width

---

## Tips ao gerar:
1. **Especifique verde escuro**: "dark forest green background", "dark teal background"
2. **Se ficar moderno demais**: peça "vintage editorial style", "art deco illustration"
3. **Se as cores não saírem**: "cream, gold, and warm tones on dark green"
4. **Se faltar botanicals**: "add sunflowers, butterflies, botanical illustrations"
5. **Se pessoas ficarem weird**: "illustrated faces" em vez de fotografias realistas
