# Prompts Refinados para DALL-E — Colagens ISR App

**Base de Estilo:** Editorial collage com fotografia preto-e-branco + elementos coloridos geométricos + flores vibrantes + linhas douradas

---

## 1. HERO / PRÓXIMO PASSO — "Sua Aula é HOJE"
```
Editorial collage artwork. Black and white close-up of young woman's face (profile view, left side of composition), serious but approachable expression. Vibrant coral and peach peonies/garden roses (right side, large, fresh). Lavender geometric circle (soft, background element, center-left). Golden wavy lines (elegant, flowing). Cream/beige background. Magazine editorial aesthetic. High detail, sophisticated. Warm, professional yet accessible. 16:9 aspect ratio.
```

## 2. EXERCÍCIO / ATIVIDADES — "My Timeline"
```
Editorial collage. Black and white photograph of hands writing on notebook (upper portion, focused). Scattered watercolor botanical elements: green leaves, delicate stems (organic, flowing around). Rose and coral accent shapes (soft, translucent). Purple/lavender geometric rectangle or bar (subtle, right side). Fine gold line details. Cream background. Square or slightly rectangular aspect ratio. Clean, minimalist, contemporary.
```

Small silhouette of person (side profile, thinking pose, minimal detail, background). Sage green and coral accents. Contemporary, tech-forward but warm aesthetic. Cream/light background. Square aspect ratio.

## Generation Settings (DALL-E):
- **Style**: Magazine editorial / contemporary collage art
- **Quality**: HD, high detail, sophisticated
- **Color Palette**: Coral (#E07856), Rose/Blush (#D4A5A5), Lavender (#B8A8D9), Sage Green (#2A9D8F), Gold (#D4A574), Cream/Beige background
- **Tone**: Warm, inviting, professional, accessible, forward-thinking
- **Composition**: Asymmetrical, layered, playful but refined
- **Format**: PNG, minimum 1200px width, high resolution

---

## Tips ao gerar:
1. Se a primeira tentativa não ficar perfeita, **refine**: "mais flores", "menos pessoas", "mais linhas douradas"
2. Gere **2-3 variações** de cada (escolha a melhor)
3. Se ficar muito cartoonesco, peça: "editorial magazine style, sophisticated"
4. Se as cores não saírem certas, especifique: "coral, lavender, sage green, cream background"
